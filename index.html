Fidget

YS: CAD-designed Fidgets WS: Your 3D-printed fidgets + a Fidget Box!

How does this work?

Use any CAD designing software(such as Onshape or KiCad, for example) to design a fidget. Track your time using Lapse and submit to earn prizes!

Ship your project and receive your 3d printed fidget or a grant to print it yourself! Ship 5 hours or more and earn a Fidget Box, which includes your 3d printed fidget along with a few other fidgets!

FAQ

Can I participate?

Anyone between the ages of 13-18 can participate!

When does this program launch?

Hopefully soon! We're not sponsored yet, so make sure to RSVP and join the channel for updates!

What is a YSWS?

YSWS stands for "You Ship, We Ship" — a Hack Club program where you build and ship cool projects, and in return you get real rewards.

Can I use AI?

Nope, sorry. Ask in #fidget if you need help or ideas, and look out for a beginner's guide coming soon!

I'm a beginner! Can I still participate even if I don't know anything about CAD?

Of course! Look out for our beginner's guide coming soon, and feel free to ask any questions in #fidget!

Is it completely free to participate?

Yes! It is completely free to participate for high schoolers.

I have more questions!

Don't stress! Just ask your questions in #fidget :)
